Development chronology

Follow the build, update by update.

This is the historical feed for the Saab 340 B project, shown newest first. Follow the latest cockpit work at the top, then move downward through simulator validation, engineering, and the original geometry.

01
Foundation update Model geometry

Building the Saab 340 B shape from the inside out.

The first milestone focused on the aircraft's underlying geometry: fuselage proportions, wing roots, nacelles, propeller assemblies, landing gear, and the dense set of small details that make the Saab 340 B recognizable. These Blender views capture the model while it was still being assembled and inspected as a working structure.

02
Engineering update Project systems

Turning the model into a maintainable aircraft project.

Once the geometry was in place, the project moved into its working development structure. Source control, aircraft configuration, animation files, cameras, and package folders became part of the daily workflow. This step matters because a convincing model needs a reliable system behind the visuals.

03
Integration update Simulator validation

Taking the Saab 340 B from the workbench into the sky.

The next milestone was to load the aircraft into the simulator and validate the model in motion. The flight views helped confirm the exterior silhouette, propeller behavior, landing gear stance, and basic flight presentation from multiple angles.

04
Identity update BeyondSkyBlue livery

Giving the aircraft a recognizable BeyondSkyBlue presence.

With the aircraft moving reliably in the simulator, the visual identity could take center stage. The BeyondSkyBlue livery, fuselage mark, tail treatment, and close exterior details now make the model feel like a real product line rather than a generic aircraft asset.

05
Current update 25 October 2026

85% ready for release: cockpit systems are the active focus.

85% release ready

The Saab 340 B is now approximately 85% ready for release. The exterior, simulator presentation, and core aircraft structure are in place. Current work is concentrated on improving the cockpit systems: the pilot-friendly tablet EFB gauge, cockpit displays, navigation workflow, system interactions, and the final details that make the model feel complete from the pilot's seat.

Exterior model: complete Simulator integration: active Cockpit systems: improving now
06
Latest development update 26 October 2026

87% done: autopilot and EFB refinement are in progress.

87% in development

Development is ongoing and the Saab 340 B is now approximately 87% complete. The current focus is on improving the autopilot and pilot-friendly EFB, adding more repaint variants, and delivering the small cockpit finishing touches that make the aircraft feel polished and enjoyable to fly.

Autopilot: improving EFB: improving Repaints and cockpit polish: active
