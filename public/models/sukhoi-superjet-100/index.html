Development chronology

The Superjet build starts with a clean trail.

This page is dedicated to the Sukhoi Superjet 100 development story. New progress notes can be added at the top as the project moves forward.

01
FoundationReference board

Lock the aircraft's modern regional-jet proportions.

Collect side, front, ramp, and air-to-air references for the fuselage, wing sweep, engine nacelles, tailplane, doors, and window rhythm.

02
Shape passExterior detail

Translate the jet's geometry into a precise model brief.

The exterior pass will focus on the nose, wing roots, engine inlets, landing gear, control surfaces, and the visual relationships that make the aircraft recognizable at a glance.

03
Next implementation focusDevelopment roadmap

Cockpit systems and simulator validation are next.

Roadmap active

The next steps are to define the flight-deck workflow, EFB surface, autopilot behavior, lighting, repaint direction, and the validation checkpoints that will guide the Superjet toward a polished release.
